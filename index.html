Our Projects

What we've been working on.

From plastic waste to renewable energy, these are some of the ways children are learning to build solutions for their communities.

1 / 5
IJANIKIN COMMUNITY (LAGOS STATE)

STEM-A-CHILD CAMPAIGN 2025

Location Credence Schools, Lagos 570+ students

Activities

  • Sensitization on plastic pollution/environmental protection.
  • Recycling plastic/e-waste into functional reading lamps.
  • UN SDGs and young leadership.
  • Collaboration/problem-solving/mentorship.
  • Post-workshop assessment over 80% improved awareness/recycling habits.
  • Peer-to-peer sustainability monitoring.
1 / 5
UGWUAJI COMMUNITY (ENUGU STATE)

Waste-to-Wealth STEM Workshop

Noble Whyte International School, Enugu 100+ students

Activities

  • Plastic pollution awareness.
  • Plastic/e-waste reading lamps.
  • Young leadership.
  • Peer collaboration/environmental responsibility.
  • Post-workshop survey over 80% improved awareness/recycling habits.
  • Peer-to-peer monitoring.

OBIAGU COMMUNITY (ENUGU STATE)

Wind Energy Workshop for Rural Kids

Uzommiri Study Center, Enugu 50+ girls

Activities

  • Mini windmill models.
  • Leadership session.
  • Certificates.
  • Teamwork.
  • Survey 90% increased interest in STEM, 85% better renewable energy understanding.
  • Sustainability peer-led learning circles.
  • Ongoing mentorship via Uzommiri Study Center.
  • Resource hub.
