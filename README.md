# Kids in LET 🌱

I started Kids in LET because I believe children shouldn't have to wait until they're older to start solving problems in their communities.

Kids in LET equips children in underserved Nigerian communities with climate technology skills to tackle plastic pollution and climate change. We use hands-on learning to introduce young people to environmental sustainability, coding, robotics and STEM.

**Our impact:** We've reached over **1,000 children across Nigeria and Namibia**, helping young people connect what they learn with the environmental challenges around them.

## What We Do

- **Climate education:** Help children understand climate change, plastic pollution and environmental sustainability.
- **Climate technology:** Introduce them to coding, robotics and STEM through practical activities.
- **Hands-on workshops:** Give children opportunities to experiment, build and learn by doing.
- **Community problem-solving:** Encourage young people to use their skills and creativity to address problems in their communities.

## Why I Started It

I believe children can be part of solving some of the world's biggest problems if we give them the knowledge, tools and opportunity to start.

That's why I started Kids in LET: to make technology and environmental education practical, accessible and relevant to the communities these children live in.

Clone the repository:

```bash

cd kidsinlet
```

Open `index.html` in your browser, or use Live Server in Visual Studio Code.
